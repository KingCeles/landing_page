What is Re0 About
  • The Plot: Ordinary modern teenager Subaru Natsuki is suddenly transported to a high-fantasy world with no special combat skills or magical prowess—except for a curse called "Return by Death."
  • The Conflict: Whenever Subaru dies, time rewinds to an arbitrary "checkpoint" in the past, allowing him to retain his memories and try to prevent fatal outcomes.
  • The Core Arc: Driven by his devotion to the half-elf candidate Emilia and the companions he meets, Subaru must endure immense psychological and physical trauma, navigating deadly political conspiracies, witch cultists, and monstrous beasts through trial, error, and sacrifice.
